Fair Fills for a Sub-Second Chain

RECALL is an order book contract for Monad. Quotes and cancels apply the moment they land, trades that take liquidity wait one block, and a cancel only beats a waiting trade when a signed price proves the quote went stale.

Cancel-First Queue

Posts and cancels apply in the block they land. Takes wait one block, so a maker's cancel reaches the book before a sniper's take, whatever fee the sniper pays.

Learn more about the queue

Price-Gated Cancel

A cancel only beats a waiting take if its signed price moved past the market's threshold since the quote was posted. No move, no jump: the fair trade fills.

Learn more about the gate

Bounded Settlement

Each call settles a capped amount of old work, oldest first. Fees stay predictable and nobody can stuff the queue to stall everyone else.

Learn more about settlement

Why Monad Makes It Practical

Fee priority is Monad's default ordering and fees are charged on the gas limit. A one-block wait costs about 0.4 seconds, so the rule fits in a plain contract with nothing changed in the chain.
